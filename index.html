About

How a yellow butterfly led me here

I grew up inland, far from the ocean. The first time I swam in the sea — off a beach near Beijing, age eight — I walked out of the water, and a yellow butterfly settled lightly on my head. In that moment the ocean was boundless to me: beautiful, fragile, and full of life at once.

I later moved to Phuket for middle school, five minutes from the sea — coral bleaching, plastic on the beaches, eroding coastlines. I started beach clean-ups, led the Eco club, and never stopped. That habit became my career: at CLP Power I built emission-tracking Power BI dashboards and a RAG AI agent (document-matching accuracy from 33% to 66%); at Westlake I co-authored a UCST carbon-flux framework, posted on SSRN (2026); my team reached the HK Tech 300 semi-finals.

"We are the world's creators — and its destroyers. That is not the world I have in mind, and we don't have time."
At the LNG terminal during my CLP Power internship.
